Why "Shibori"

Named after the Japanese art ofshaping cloth by hand.

Shibori is a resist-dye craft. Cloth is folded, bound or wrapped before it meets the dye, so no two pieces come out the same. We work the same way: slowly, by hand, in small numbers.
